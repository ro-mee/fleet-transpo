## What I learned

- `node --check` rejects `.jsx` (`ERR_UNKNOWN_FILE_EXTENSION`) — only valid for `.js`; JSX needs the bundler/eslint path.
- `useTheme()` (`mobile/lib/theme-context.js`) exposes no `isDark` key — six screens destructure it as `undefined`. Left untouched (behavior fix, not dead code).
