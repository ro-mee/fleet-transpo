# 2026-08-31

## Major Milestones & Architectural Refactors

### 1. Canonical Directional Routes & Location Identity (Commit `90b9504`)
- **Database Migrations (`076_routes_integrity.sql` → `080_backfill_hotel_location_identity.sql`)**:
  - Enforced relational foreign keys (`origin_location_id`, `destination_location_id` $\rightarrow$ `locations(location_id)`).
  - Enforced unique constraint on canonical endpoint pairs `(origin_location_id, destination_location_id)`.
  - Normalized bidirectional arrow notations (`↔` replaced by `→`) across route titles.
  - Implemented route immutability locking: endpoints lock once referenced in dispatches or completed trips; historical routes deactivate (`status = 'Inactive'`) rather than delete.
  - Preserved canonical hotel location identity (`080_backfill_hotel_location_identity.sql`) so hotel renames update in place while physical moves version and retire legacy geography.
- **Route Resolver Service (`src/services/route-resolver.service.js`)**:
  - Centralized route matcher and TomTom metric resolver used by booking intake, dispatch auto-create, and AI dispatch recommendations.
- **Automated Verification**:
  - Created `scripts/verify-route-integrity.mjs` ensuring zero orphaned endpoints and validated constraints.
- **Synced Vault Documentation**:
  - Created `Capstone/02 - Features/Routes.md` and updated `Capstone/03 - Database/Migrations/Migrations.md`.

### 2. Trip-Scoped & Status-Aware Live GPS Tracking (Commit `abdc001`)
- **Trip-Scoped GPS Ingestion (`src/lib/gps.js`)**:
  - Hardened `/api/mobile/driver/gps` and `/api/mobile/driver/trips/[id]/gps` to validate active trip state.
  - Pings are strictly appended to `gpstracking` only when a driver is actively executing an `In Progress` trip, preventing idle depot stops from polluting trip breadcrumbs.
- **Connection Health & Staleness Detection**:
  - Implemented `isStaleGps()` with a 3-minute threshold (`GPS_STALE_THRESHOLD_MS = 180000 ms`).
  - Active vehicles without a recent fix are flagged with "Stale/Disconnected" status and last-ping duration.
- **Web Live Map Overhaul (`/tracking/live-map` & `src/components/maps/live-locations-map.jsx`)**:
  - Added status filtering chips (*All*, *In Progress*, *Dispatched*, *Assigned*, *Stale/Disconnected*).
  - Color-coded map markers (Emerald for In Progress, Amber for Dispatched/Assigned, Slate for Stale/Inactive).
  - Isolated breadcrumb trails exclusively to the selected active trip, eliminating visual polyline clutter.
  - Added driver telemetry cards with live speed, heading, and destination ETA.
- **Mobile Lifecycle Synchronization**:
  - `mobile/lib/background-tracking.js` mounts and unmounts background location listeners strictly aligned with trip lifecycle events.
- **Synced Vault Documentation**:
  - Updated `Capstone/02 - Features/Tracking.md`.
