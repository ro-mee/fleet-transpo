# 2026-08-23

## What I did today

- Implemented **Circular Reveal Theme Transitions** via the browser **View Transition API** (`document.startViewTransition`) paired with a CSS `clip-path` expansion on `::view-transition-new(root)`.
- Updated `src/hooks/use-theme.js` to accept pointer/click events, calculating the exact click origin `(clientX, clientY)` and maximum radius to the furthest screen corner.
- Configured `src/app/globals.css` with dedicated `::view-transition-old(root)` and `::view-transition-new(root)` stacking rules to eliminate default cross-fade and let the incoming theme expand over the old theme.
- Wired click events in `TopNav` (`src/components/layout/app-shell.jsx`) and `/settings/general` (`src/app/(dashboard)/settings/general/page.js`).
- Verified full test suite (`374/374` vitest tests passing) and ensured graceful fallback for reduced-motion and non-supporting environments.

- Redesigned the **Dispatch Scheduling Board** (`/dispatch/calendar`) into a high-end operations-grade board:
  - **Multi-Level Information Cards**: Enriched cards displaying scheduled time window, guest name & pax, assigned driver, assigned vehicle make/model & plate, route breadcrumbs, VIP/priority badges, and unassigned status callouts.
  - **Operational Detail Drawer** (`CalendarDetailDrawer`): Interactive slide-over modal presenting the complete dossier on click (route pins, schedule comparisons, driver license & vehicle capacity, and conflict diagnostics).
  - **Live "Now" Indicator**: Real-time glowing red time line with live clock badge for immediate dispatch situational awareness.
  - **Smart All-Day Restrictions**: Expandable/collapsible grouped counters for Driver Leave, Rest Days, and PMS/Maintenance.
  - **Operational KPI Strip & Filter Toolbar**: Live metric counters (`Total Bookings`, `Active / Soon`, `Needs Assignment`, `Conflicts`, `Out of Service`) paired with debounced search and type filters.
  - **Density Modes**: Added Comfortable and Compact density toggles.
