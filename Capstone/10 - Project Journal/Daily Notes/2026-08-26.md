# 2026-08-26

## What was built / changed

- Created **`ThemeToggle` (`src/components/ui/theme-toggle.jsx`)**:
  - Reversible SVG morphing & rotation micro-interaction:
    - **Light $\rightarrow$ Dark:** Crescent Moon smoothly morphs into a sun, spins $90^\circ$, and sun rays expand outward.
    - **Dark $\rightarrow$ Light (pabalik):** Spins $0^\circ$ back, rays contract inward, and mask slides back to recreate the crescent Moon.
  - Button isolated from view-transition background snapshots via `view-transition-name: theme-toggle-btn` and `z-index: 10000`.
- Implemented **Asymmetric Directional View Transitions** in `src/hooks/use-theme.js` & `src/app/globals.css`:
  - **Light $\rightarrow$ Dark:** Screen wave expands outward (`circle(0px) \rightarrow circle(endRadius)`) originating from the button.
  - **Dark $\rightarrow$ Light (Pabalik):** Screen wave contracts inward (`circle(endRadius) \rightarrow circle(0px)`) back into the button, sucking the dark veil back in and revealing the light page underneath (hindi na pakalat).
- Integrated `ThemeToggle` into **`TopNav` in `src/components/layout/app-shell.jsx`**.
- Documented component in `Capstone/05 - Codebase/Components.md` and `Capstone/04 - Architecture/Frontend.md`.
- All 443 unit tests passing; clean ESLint verification.
